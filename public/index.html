01 — The beginning

Easy to learn.
Impossible to exhaust.

Two players take turns placing black and white stones on a grid. The aim is to surround more territory than your opponent. That’s the starting point — the rest is up to you.

02 — Try it out

Put a stone on the board.

Explore the game hands-on: play both sides, practice legal moves, and see how stones capture one another. Open the demo board.
